Preparation

SDI DPV

What to bring to the course and which forms you need beforehand. Print the list or save it as a PDF to tick things off.

What to bring

Suit and thermal protection

In a wetsuit you need neoprene boots, and the base layer, socks and p-valve kit stay at home.

  • Drysuit and undersuit, or a wetsuit suited to the water temperature
  • Base layer
  • Warm socks, for example from Fourth Element
  • Hood
  • Gloves, wet and dry, plus liner gloves
  • Two beanies, one tends to go missing
  • For the p-valve: condom catheters with click adapter, or She-P or Dive-P, plus adhesive and remover

Dive gear

  • Your dive gear, backmount or sidemount
  • Dive computer

Scooter

If you don't have your own scooter, you can rent one.

  • Scooter (DPV)

Tools and safety

  • Cutting tool
  • Wet notes with pencil
  • Spool with at least 30 m of line, and a buoy
  • Analyser and tools, if you have them

Personal

  • Something to eat and drink
  • Personal medication

Paperwork before the course

  • Diver Medical Participant Questionnaire PDFFill it in before the first session, including before the theory. If you answer yes to question 3, 5 or 10, or to any question on page 2, you need a doctor's clearance on page 3, so leave time for that.
  • Liability ReleaseFor reading ahead. You sign it at the first session, together with everyone in the class.

Please don't send the medical by email or through the contact form. Bring it filled in to the first session.
